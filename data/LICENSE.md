# Data license

The data committed under `data/` (overrides in `data/overrides/`, the attribution
log in `data/attribution/`) is made available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

It refers to and corrects data derived from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap
contributors, ODbL 1.0), so it uses the same license. Photo ids in the attribution
log point to Mapillary and KartaView images, which remain under their own licenses
(CC BY-SA 4.0); the images themselves are not stored here.

Generated tile builds (`data/builds/`, not committed) are produced works of
OpenStreetMap, City of Chicago and USGS data; display the attribution shown in the
viewer wherever they are published.
